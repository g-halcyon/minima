# Minima Commercial License Licensing Guide

Minima is licensed under the **[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0](LICENSE)**. 

Free use, building, modifying, and distribution are permitted for **non-commercial, personal, hobby, research, and educational purposes**.

---

## Requiring a Commercial License

A **Commercial License** is required if you wish to:

- Embed Minima or its C++ core into a commercial or paid software product.
- Re-brand or distribute Minima as a monetized browser or product.
- Use Minima within a commercial business, enterprise, or revenue-generating operation.
- Integrate Minima into a proprietary closed-source application without adhering to non-commercial source terms.

---

## Inquiries & Granting Commercial Licenses

Commercial licenses are custom tailored based on distribution scale, enterprise integration, or proprietary rights needed.

To request a commercial license agreement:
- Contact **`g-halcyon`** via GitHub: [https://github.com/g-halcyon](https://github.com/g-halcyon)
- Open a commercial license request issue or direct message on GitHub.
